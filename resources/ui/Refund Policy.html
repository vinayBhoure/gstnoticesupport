Refund Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026 Last updated: 14 March 2026

Professional fees are payable upfront. This policy explains when a refund is available and how to claim it.

On this page
1. Principle 2. Full Refund 3. Partial Refund 4. No Refund 5. Govt. Fees & Pre-deposit 6. How to Request 7. Processing Timeline 8. Grievance Redressal

1. Principle

We charge a fixed professional fee that compensates our Chartered Accountants and Advocates for time spent reviewing your case, preparing drafts, and supporting filing. Once that work has begun, the fee is not generally refundable. Refund eligibility depends on the stage at which you cancel.

2. Full Refund

You are eligible for a 100% refund of the professional fee if:

  • You cancel before document review begins — typically within 4 hours of payment
  • We are unable to take on your case after the initial review (e.g., conflict of interest, scope mismatch)
  • We fail to deliver the draft within the published SLA without prior intimation, despite complete documents from your side

3. Partial Refund

A partial refund (typically 50%) may be granted if you cancel after document review but before draft delivery. The deduction covers the review effort already invested.

4. No Refund

No refund is available once any of the following has occurred:

  • The draft response, appeal, or representation has been shared with you
  • The filing has been submitted on the GST portal on your authorisation
  • You stopped responding to clarification requests for more than 7 working days
  • Documents shared turn out to be forged, materially false, or in violation of these Terms

5. Government Fees & Pre-deposit

Government fees (filing fees, pre-deposit for Commissioner Appeal at 10%, GSTAT at 20%, court fees, courier, notarisation) are not part of our professional fee and are not refundable by us under any circumstance. Recovery of pre-deposits is subject to the order of the appellate authority.

6. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email hello@gstnoticesupport.in from your registered address with your case reference number
  2. State the reason for cancellation
  3. Our team reviews stage of work and confirms eligibility in writing within 2 working days

7. Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment instrument:

  • UPI / Net banking: within 3–5 working days
  • Credit / debit card: within 5–7 working days, subject to issuing bank

Bank-side delays are outside our control. We share the gateway reference number once the refund is initiated.

8. Grievance Redressal

If a refund decision is unsatisfactory, escalate in writing to our Grievance Officer:

Ananya Rao
grievance@gstnoticesupport.in

The Grievance Officer responds within 30 days as required by the IT Rules, 2011.
