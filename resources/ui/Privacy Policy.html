Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026 Last updated: 14 March 2026 Compliance: DPDP Act 2023, IT Rules 2011

This Privacy Policy describes how GST Notice Support Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us") collects, processes, stores, and discloses personal information when you use our website and services.

On this page
1. Scope & Applicability 2. Data We Collect 3. Purpose of Processing 4. Sharing & Disclosure 5. Retention 6. Security Measures 7. Your Rights 8. Cookies & Tracking 9. Minors 10. Grievance Officer

1. Scope & Applicability

This Policy applies to all visitors and registered users of gstnoticesupport.in and to any service engagement booked through the portal.

By accessing the website or creating an account, you consent to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use.

2. Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Identity & contact

  • Name, designation, business name, email, mobile number
  • GSTIN, PAN, and entity address you provide when booking a service
  • Government-issued ID submitted for KYC, where required

2.2 Case documents

  • GST notices (ASMT-10, DRC-01, APL-01, APL-05 and related forms)
  • Supporting documents you upload — returns, ledgers, invoices, departmental orders
  • Communications between you and our professionals

2.3 Usage data

  • IP address, device, browser, pages visited, time spent
  • Cookies — see Section 8 for detail

3. Purpose of Processing

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  1. To deliver the GST litigation service you have engaged us for
  2. To verify your identity and prevent fraud or misuse
  3. To send transactional updates (case status, draft ready, payment receipts) by email, SMS, and WhatsApp
  4. To comply with statutory record-keeping under the CGST Act, Income Tax Act, and Companies Act
  5. To improve our website and service workflow — strictly through aggregated, de-identified analytics

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We share information only as follows:

  • Assigned CA or Advocate — the professional handling your case sees the documents necessary to prepare your response
  • Tax authorities and GST portal — your authorised filings, on your explicit instruction
  • Service providers — payment gateway, cloud storage, email and SMS infrastructure, subject to binding data processing agreements
  • Regulators and courts — when required by law, summons, or valid order of a competent authority

We never share your business documents with marketing platforms, ad networks, or third-party analytics that profile individuals.

5. Retention

Case files are retained for 8 years from the closure of the engagement — the statutory record-retention period under Section 36 of the CGST Act, 2017. Auth and account metadata are retained until you request deletion.

6. Security Measures

  • TLS 1.3 encryption in transit; AES-256 at rest
  • Role-based access — only your assigned professional can view your case
  • Audit logs of every document access and download
  • Annual third-party security review

Despite reasonable safeguards, no transmission over the internet is fully secure. We recommend you do not share credentials with anyone.

7. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may at any time:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request erasure, subject to retention requirements above
  • Withdraw consent for future processing
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights in case of incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, write to our Grievance Officer (Section 10).

8. Cookies & Tracking

We use strictly-necessary cookies to keep you logged in and to remember your booking preferences. We use first-party analytics cookies to understand aggregate usage. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

9. Minors

Our services are intended for businesses and individuals 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
