Fees & Payment

4 questions

Fixed fees per service: ASMT-10 Reply ₹2,999 · SCN Reply ₹5,999 · Commissioner Appeal ₹9,999 · GSTAT Appeal ₹14,999. All fees are inclusive of professional charges.

See Full Pricing

Commissioner Appeal and GSTAT Appeal require a statutory pre-deposit — 10% and 20% of the disputed tax respectively — paid directly by you to the GST portal. This is a legal requirement under the GST Act. It is not our fee and is not processed through this platform. We guide you through the pre-deposit process step by step.

Our fee covers the professional service of drafting and filing — not the outcome of the case. Fees are non-refundable once draft preparation begins. This is stated clearly before any payment is taken. Legal outcomes depend on the facts, law, and the authority's judgement — no professional can guarantee a specific result.
