Sub: Reply to Assessment Notice under Section 61 read with Section 73 of the CGST Act, 2017 for FY 2024–25 — GSTIN: 29ABCDE1234F1Z5

Respected Sir/Madam,

We write in response to the ASMT-10 notice dated 01 June 2026 (Notice No. GST/ASMT-10/BLS/2026/0412) pointing to discrepancies in Input Tax Credit (ITC) claims for the period April 2024 – March 2025.

After a thorough review, we submit the following factual and legal response:

1. Discrepancy in ITC claims — GSTR-2A vs. GSTR-3B

The observed difference of ₹3,82,450 arises primarily from timing differences in vendor filings. Our GSTR-2A figures for Q3 FY 2024–25 were reconciled as on 31 March 2025. Supporting reconciliation statements are enclosed as Annexure A.

2. Legal position under Section 16(2)(c) — CGST Act

As per Section 16(2)(c), ITC eligibility is contingent on the tax having been paid by the supplier. We have obtained vendor declarations confirming tax payment for all disputed invoices. These declarations are enclosed as Annexure B.
