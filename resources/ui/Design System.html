Design System

Banking-grade, government-portal-familiar. Built for CAs, advocates, and SME owners who just received a tax notice. Calm, sparse, institutional — restraint signals confidence. Every screen inherits these locked tokens and shared components.

Principles, in priority order. 1 — Trust before conversion. 2 — One primary decision per screen. 3 — Restraint signals confidence. 4 — Specificity beats emotion. 5 — Readability is a trust signal.

Interaction Rules

Every interactive element carries explicit default, hover, focus, active, disabled states.

One primary, never two

Pair one filled primary with one outline secondary. Adjacent primaries cause decision paralysis.

Whole-card clickable

When a card represents a single choice, the entire card is the target — not just the inner link.

Single-item accordions

FAQs expand one item at a time.

Disabled stays visible

Show a short reason rather than silently hiding a blocked action.

Single-column forms

Large fields, inline validation, errors specific and adjacent to the field.

Tables → cards on mobile

Tables collapse to stacked cards below the breakpoint; admin tables may scroll horizontally.

Trust & Accessibility

Trust patterns and accessibility are not optional polish — they are the product. The audience is skeptical and high-stakes.

Mandatory legal identity

Footer carries legal name, CIN, GSTIN, address, grievance officer, SSL, policies. Refund policy stated transparently.

Reassurance near every upload

Confidentiality + encryption claim adjacent to the highest-stakes interaction; confirm with a case reference.

Tap targets ≥ 48px

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus ring on every element, never color alone for state.

No urgency theatre

No countdown timers, "X viewing now" badges, early pop-ups, or "guaranteed win" language.

No fake people

No Western stock photos, no avatars for the team, no autoplay video with sound.

India-specific trust

UPI named in payment contexts, tap-to-call on CTAs, WhatsApp as an evaluation channel with human handoff.
