# Service Workflow Analysis

## Overview
The document outlines the workflow and operational process for four GST-related legal and compliance services:

1. GST Appellate Tribunal Appeal
2. Commissioner Appeal
3. Show Cause Notice (SCN) Reply
4. ASMT-10 Reply

The workflow across all services follows a structured 4-step process:
- Document Collection
- Draft Preparation
- Client Review & Confirmation
- Portal Filing

---

# Service 1 – GST Appellate Tribunal Appeal

## Required Documents
Clients are required to share:
- Show Cause Notice (SCN)
- SCN Reply
- Demand Order (OIO / Order of Adjudicating Authority)
- First Appeal & Order (OIA / Commissioner Appeal Order)
- Other relevant documents / evidence

## Draft Preparation
The expert team prepares:
- Statement of Facts
- Grounds of Appeal
- Prayer
- APL-05 structured draft format

The draft is shared with the client for review.

## Review & Confirmation
Process includes:
- Client reviews and confirms the draft
- GSTAT portal login credentials are shared securely
- Payment of statutory pre-deposit as per GST Act

## Portal Filing
The team completes filing on the GSTAT portal after final confirmation.

## Additional Notes
### Note 1
Before payment, only a partial preview of the draft appeal is shared with the client. Full access is provided after fee payment.

### Note 2
Client mobile number and email ID are mandatory.

### Note 3
The team may contact the client during draft preparation for clarification or additional documents.

# Suggested Workflow Improvements

## 1. Add Timeline Expectations
Mention estimated timelines for:
- Draft preparation
- Review cycles
- Filing completion

This improves customer confidence.

## 2. Add Document Checklist Format
A downloadable checklist or upload dashboard can reduce back-and-forth communication.

## 3. Introduce Status Tracking
Possible workflow stages:
- Documents Received
- Draft In Progress
- Awaiting Review
- Filing Completed

This improves client experience.

## 4. Add Secure Document Upload System
Instead of sharing documents manually:
- Use encrypted upload portals
- Role-based access
- Audit logs

## 5. Improve Terminology Consistency
Some sections mention GSTAT portal even for non-tribunal services. This may confuse users if different portals/processes are actually used.
